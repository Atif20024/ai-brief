# Design references for this site

Restyle only within these rules, in this order of authority:

1. taste-skill (Leonxlnx) — https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill — the system: ground #F3F3F1, white cards with a 1px hairline (ink at 12%), no shadow at rest, lift 2px on hover (140ms in / 200ms out, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)), radius 20, card padding 28, card title 20/500, card text 16/24 in 84% ink (never small grey text), labels 13/500 sentence case, one accent (blue), never violet, header fixed and opaque, nothing else sticky, every card row full.
2. Anthropic frontend-design skill — one typeface (Inter), hierarchy by weight, no all-caps labels, no decorative chrome.
3. Emil Kowalski's Apple design skill — feedback on press, respect reduced motion.

Layout: tier bands (Must see / Should see / Good to know) tinted lightly, label in the left 20% with a coloured dot, cards in a 6-column collage on the right: first row 4+2, then 2+2+2, leftovers widened so rows always close.
